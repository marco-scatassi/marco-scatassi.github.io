// Background & Story

Mathematical Foundations meet Modern Data Engineering

My journey began with a Bachelor’s degree in Mathematics at the University of Bologna. Developing rigorous analytical thinking and formal problem-solving laid the ground for my transition into advanced computing and statistics.

To apply mathematical models to real-world challenges, I completed a Master’s degree in Data Science at Università degli Studi di Milano-Bicocca, focusing on Distributed Data Architectures, Machine Learning, and Big Data Technologies.

Currently working in Data Consulting & Engineering (Nimbus Intelligence), I help organizations build scalable, maintainable, and efficient cloud data pipelines using tools like Snowflake, dbt, Python, and PySpark.
