// Profile & Background

Academic Foundations & Technical Journey

I earned my Bachelor's Degree in Mathematics at the University of Bologna, where I built a solid base in theoretical logic, probability, linear algebra, and mathematical analysis.

To direct these analytical tools toward large-scale computational challenges, I completed my Master's Degree in Data Science at Università degli Studi di Milano-Bicocca. My studies centered around big data architectures, distributed computing with PySpark, machine learning algorithms, and software development practices.

I work as a Data Engineering Trainee / Consultant at Nimbus Intelligence in Milan, designing modular, scalable data transformation flows with Snowflake, dbt, Python, and SQL.

"Focusing on clean logic, maintainable transformation models, and robust pipeline engineering."
